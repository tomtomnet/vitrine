# System logos

The logos vitrine shows for the system a VM runs (`app/src/ui/osicons.cpp`),
drawn in white on a square of the brand's colour. An icon of the desktop's
theme wins when it has one: `distributor-logo-NAME`, or a distribution's own
(Fedora's `fedora-logo-icon`). Systems without a logo here get the logo of
their family (Linux, Windows) or the theme's computer icon.

The logos are trademarks of their owners. vitrine shows them to tell which
system a VM runs; they are not covered by vitrine's license.

## vitrine's own

windows.svg: four squares, vitrine's drawing, GPL-2.0-or-later like
vitrine. Simple Icons no longer has Microsoft's logos. Windows is a
trademark of Microsoft.

## Not here

Fedora's logo: Simple Icons has it under Fedora's own terms (its trademark
guidelines), which do not fit. vitrine uses the system's `fedora-logo-icon`
(Fedora's fedora-logos package) or the theme's `distributor-logo-fedora`,
else a letter on Fedora's blue. Gentoo's (CC-BY-SA-2.5) likewise: a letter.

Debian's (CC-BY-SA-3.0, or LGPL-3.0-or-later), NixOS's (CC-BY-4.0) and
Rocky Linux's (CC-BY-SA-4.0) are not bundled either: they fit GPLv3, not
GPLv2, and vitrine's binary is GPL-2.0-or-later. The theme's logo is used
when it has one (`debian-logo`, `distributor-logo-nixos`,
`distributor-logo-rocky`), else a letter on the brand's colour.
